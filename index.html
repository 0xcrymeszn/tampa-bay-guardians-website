01 Understanding guardianship

What is guardianship?

Guardianship is a legal relationship in which a court appoints someone to help make personal, healthcare, or financial decisions for a person who cannot safely make those decisions on their own.

It is about protecting a person’s well-being while respecting their voice, preferences, and independence as much as possible.
